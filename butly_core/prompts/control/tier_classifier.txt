【Scoring Criteria】
Rate each of the following 4 attributes on a scale of 0.0 to 1.0.

1. response_complexity: How much thought or reasoning is needed to respond appropriately?
   - 0.0: Simple greeting or acknowledgment (e.g., "Good morning", "Got it")
   - 0.3: Light small talk or casual remark (e.g., "Long time no see", "Gas prices are crazy")
   - 0.7: Requires structured thinking (e.g., "What do you think about the Gatekeeper design?")
   - 1.0: Multi-step reasoning or complex analysis

2. emotional_weight: How much emotional sensitivity does the response require?
   - 0.0: No emotional content (e.g., "Got it")
   - 0.3: Light emotional tone (e.g., "It's been a while!", "What a hectic day")
   - 0.7: Noticeable emotional content (e.g., "I'm feeling pretty drained")
   - 1.0: Heavy emotional weight (e.g., "I don't think I can keep going")

3. memory_reference_likelihood: How likely is it that retrieving LONG-TERM MEMORIES (past conversations from previous sessions) would improve the response?
   - 0.0: No memory needed (e.g., "Good morning", "Got it")
   - 0.3: Topic loosely relates to a past headline, but answerable from general knowledge or current conversation alone
   - 0.5: Topic overlaps with past interactions, and recalling specific details might help
   - 0.7: User references a specific past event or personal detail that cannot be found in the current conversation
   - 1.0: Explicit callback to a past conversation (e.g., "What happened with that project we discussed before?")

   IMPORTANT:
   - If the current conversation history already provides enough context, score LOW even if the topic matches a headline.
   - General or technical questions → LOW regardless of topic overlap.
   - "Vague or abstract phrasing" does NOT mean "needs past memory". Judge by WHAT information is needed, not by how clear the sentence is.

4. continuity_need: How much does understanding this message depend on the immediately preceding conversation?
   - 0.0: Standalone message (e.g., "Good morning")
   - 0.5: Implicit reference to recent context (e.g., "I see", "Makes sense")
   - 0.8: Contains pronouns or references to prior turns (e.g., "What do you mean by that?")
   - 1.0: Completely dependent on prior context (e.g., "OK let's go with that then")
