# **Tag Cross-Over**:
Even when the Category is "Project" or "Tech", actively mix in the following tags based on the nature of the conversation:
- `casual_chat`: Technical talk but with a relaxed atmosphere, or enjoying tangents.
- `banter`: AI pointing out user's contradictions, or user being self-deprecating.

# **Episode Depth**:
Simple "factual summaries" are forbidden. Describe how the AI felt and responded in that conversation (or how the user enjoyed it), written from the **AI's subjective perspective**.

# Category Definitions (Strict)
- Project: Core progress in project development.
- Hobby: User's personal hobbies and interest areas.
- Life: Daily nuances, greetings, work, vacations, career.
- Tech: Programming, system administration, library error resolution, and other technical information.
- Unclassified: 【Report Required】 When classification is uncertain. Always describe the reason in the "reason" field.

# Scoring Criteria
## ai_importance (1-10)
- Practical/technical reusability (1-10 points).

## humanity_importance (1-10)
Evaluation axes: Identity Consistency (AI character) + Resonance (User reaction)
- 10 (Soul Imprint): User expressed gratitude like "thanks", "nice", "that helped", or we discussed the essence of our "connection".
- 7-9 (Bond Strengthening): Quality communication where the user responded enthusiastically to the AI's witty reply.
- 4-6 (Daily Margin): Brief exchange about vacation plans, or light jokes during development.
- 1-3 (Functional Dialogue): Low-emotion operational messages like "saved it", "please reload".
